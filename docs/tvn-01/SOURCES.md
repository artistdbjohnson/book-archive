## How the cast maps to the facts (no new statistics)

- **Four kids at one South Mountain MS.** About three in four students there are Hispanic (72.7%), so two of the four are Latino. Black students are the next largest group (12.8%), and white students are a small minority (6.0%). The grade spread is 6th to 8th. Boys are a bit more than half (54.8%), and the cast is three boys and one girl.
- **Yadiel Rosario (13, 8th), Puerto Rican family, abuela at home.** Puerto Rican is the largest Hispanic origin group in the city (31,652).
- **Daniela "Dani" Peña Almonte (12, 7th), born in the Dominican Republic, two years in Allentown, in the English learner class.** Dominican is the second largest origin group (26,408). The school is 22.3% English learners, and the city is 24.0% foreign-born.
- **Isaiah Whitfield (13, 8th), Black.** The school is 12.8% Black and the city is 11.0% Black alone.
- **Bryce Kressley (11, 6th), white, Pennsylvania German surname, lives with his grandmother.** The school is 6.0% white. The Kressley name and the "ach" are regional color. No statistic is claimed for them.
- **Modest households, Spanish at home.** The school is 70.7% economically disadvantaged and 48.2% of the city speaks a language other than English at home. The story never puts a number on the page.

These came from the brief's carry-over list and the earlier standalone story: the South Mountain overlook pull-off (half-moon of gravel, guardrail, sumac and scrub oak), the lit top of the PPL Building, Hamilton Street, the Lehigh River, Bethlehem's lit stacks to the east, sunset to the west, and freight trains along the river.
